1. **The "i"→"o" morph is weak.** In the reference (sec_02, 2.60–2.80) only the "a" of "Feitosa" is left, and it reshapes into the round "o". An "i" has no bowl to reshape. **Fix:** delete down to the final "i", then grow its dot into the "o" while the stem drops away. The storyboard also never says "Shayan" gets deleted (ref 2.60: "Tiago" goes too), so state it.

4. **The end card drops the title.** The brief says viewers should remember "Growth Partner", but from 7.80 onward only the name is on screen. **Fix:** keep the collapse but leave a small "Growth Partner" under the name, or put it under the logo.
5. **Missed beat, 3.73–3.87 (sec_03).** The head lifts and pushes toward the camera with blur as the pupil rises with an iris ring behind it. **Fix:** add the head lift and push, plus the iris ring appearing behind the pupil.
